I am a bachelor student at the Royal Danish Academy of Fine Arts in Copenhagen, specializing in oil painting and sculptural practice.

I am fascinated by magical realism and the distorted side of our reality — the absurd and what lies beneath the surface. In my works, I explore the human psyche, body, and flesh through a grotesque and corporeal expression, which becomes a physical manifestation of something that is happening inside. Often with a feminist, taboo-breaking, or critical undertone.

If you want to know more about my work or are curious about pricing, I would love to hear from you.
